# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Backfort to be a practical, respectful place for people who maintain
systems and protect data. Participation in this project should be free from
harassment, intimidation, discrimination, and needless hostility.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, incidents, and design choices in good faith.
- Be precise about risk, evidence, uncertainty, and operational impact.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, identities, and working
  environments.
- Give actionable review feedback and accept correction when evidence changes.
- Protect confidential information: redact secrets, personal data, infrastructure
  details, and customer backup content.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, slurs, sexualized language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private communications, credentials, personal information, or
  security details without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, bad-faith reports, or pressure to bypass
  safety and security controls.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or vulnerability in good
  faith.

## Reporting a concern

For conduct concerns, contact the project owner through the
[ShellHarbor GitHub profile](https://github.com/shellharbor). Use a GitHub issue
only when the matter can safely be discussed in public. Reports will be handled
as privately and fairly as the available communication channel permits.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
and do not post exploit details publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify expectations, request a correction, remove
content, temporarily limit participation, or permanently ban a participant,
depending on severity and pattern. Decisions aim to protect contributors and
users while remaining proportionate to the behavior and available evidence.
